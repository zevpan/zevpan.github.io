Working method

Not “AI everywhere.” The right control for the right work.

The common thread across the portfolio is separating stable rules, contextual AI assistance and human decision authority — then translating that operating model into something testable.

01 DiscoverMap the real operating workflow.
02 DiagnoseFind the structural failure, not just symptoms.
03 RedesignDefine the target operating model.
04 GovernSeparate deterministic, AI and human authority.
05 PrototypeMake the proposed workflow usable.
06 EvaluateMeasure system and decision quality.
Deterministic where possible.

Stable policy, state, validation, eligibility and calculations belong in explicit rules.

AI where meaning is unstructured.

Classification, summarization, evidence review and drafting benefit from contextual models.

Humans where judgement matters.

Exceptions, liability, identity, commercial decisions and external actions remain controlled.
