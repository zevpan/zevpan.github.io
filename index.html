How I work

Understand the work before changing it.

Transformation begins with how people actually complete the process: constraints, evidence, data, exceptions, and decision rights. The interface or model comes after the work is understood.

01 · DiagnoseMap current paths, handoffs, decision points, failure modes, and constraints.
02 · SpecifySet requirements, data definitions, owners, rules, and acceptance criteria.
03 · PrototypeMake the target workflow tangible in an interface and operational logic.
04 · ValidateTest task completion, edge cases, decision quality, and controls.
Use rules for stable decisions.

Validation, eligibility, state changes, calculations, and policy gates should be explicit and testable.

Use AI for ambiguous information.

Models can help interpret unstructured evidence, compare context, summarize, and prepare drafts.

Keep people accountable.

People own exceptions, consequential judgment, external communication, and final approval.

How I think about AI-enabled transformation

Design the system of work, not just the prompt.

My AI approach starts with the operating objective, policy, and tools already in place. A provider-neutral intelligence layer can coordinate bounded tasks across existing systems; it should return sources, assumptions, exceptions, and proposed work for human review.

MODEL PROVIDER AGNOSTIC
HumanIntent + policySets outcome, constraints, and decision rights.
OrchestratorPlan + routeBreaks the request into bounded tasks.
AI / automationInterpret + executeAgents use suitable models, explicit rules, and approved tools.
Existing systemsData + actionCRMs, workflow engines, files, APIs, and systems of record remain useful.
Human reviewEvidence + approvalConsequential actions return to the responsible person.
Deterministic logicState, calculations, eligibility, and policy gates belong in explicit workflow rules.
Probabilistic assistanceLLMs assist with meaning and synthesis where context is unstructured; model choice is replaceable.
Human authorityExternal messages, high-impact changes, and ambiguous exceptions remain reviewable or approval-gated.
How I use AI to build: I define the outcome, constraints, requirements, and acceptance criteria; AI accelerates research and implementation; I inspect evidence, make product decisions, test the result, and own acceptance. Language for intent. Structured UI for cognition. Agents for execution.
Professional grounding

Business context first. Systems second.

The projects draw on operational questions across travel logistics, relationship workflows, warehouse decisions, and shared team knowledge. I treat policy, data quality, human capacity, exceptions, and the system of record as part of the design, not as implementation details to discover later.
