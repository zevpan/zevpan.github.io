Case-study approach

These cases focus on the transformation decisions behind AI-enabled workflows: how the process is diagnosed, what should remain human, what should be deterministic, where AI adds genuine leverage, and how the resulting system is governed.

Case studies are anonymized where appropriate. Product-build demo data and scenarios are synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise.
