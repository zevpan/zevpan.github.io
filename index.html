The operating model

Software should understand the outcome—not require a memorized click path.

The portfolio explores a shift from interface-led work to intent-led work. Language expresses the goal; structured interfaces stay available for scanning, comparison, correction, and approval. Agents handle bounded operational tasks across the systems an organization already uses.

Intent → execution → reviewable outcome

One reusable architecture for job search, operations, relationship work, and shared knowledge.

Working method

Intent directs the work. Evidence and policy govern execution.

Each project separates repeatable rules, model-assisted work on unstructured context, and decisions that belong to a person. The product surface remains available for review and correction.

DreamName the change you want to create.
PlanMap users, process, data, and decision rights.
ExecuteBuild and orchestrate work across current systems.
ReviewInspect real task completion, exceptions, and evidence.
IterateCritique, adjust requirements, and test again.
01 DiscoverMap the real operating workflow.
02 DiagnoseFind the structural failure, not just symptoms.
03 RedesignDefine the target operating model.
04 GovernSeparate deterministic, AI and human authority.
05 PrototypeMake the proposed workflow usable.
06 EvaluateMeasure system and decision quality.
Deterministic where possible.

Stable policy, state, validation, eligibility and calculations belong in explicit rules.

AI where meaning is unstructured.

Classification, summarization, evidence review and drafting benefit from contextual models.

Humans where judgement matters.

Exceptions, liability, identity, commercial decisions and external actions remain controlled.

Human direction→AI-assisted research and analysis→Build→Inspect→Critique→Iterate
My role: set objectives and constraints, frame the process, define requirements and acceptance, make product decisions, and review the result. AI accelerates research, design, and engineering work; it does not own product judgment.
