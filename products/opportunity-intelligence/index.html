Built around the job seeker’s decisions

More than a saved-jobs list.

The product concept connects role fit, career value, relevant relationships, preparation, and follow-through in one workspace.

01

Prioritize opportunities

Review active roles using visible fit, career-value, and timing weights, with due and quiet work surfaced for attention.

02

Inspect the evidence

Compare strengths, gaps, confidence, role scope, and unresolved questions before acting.

03

Plan a proportionate next step

Consider relationship context, select a resume approach, record follow-up, or change the priority yourself.

Clear prototype boundaries

Guidance stays explainable and user controlled.

Scores support triage. They are not hiring probabilities, and a network contact does not increase a role’s quality score.

DATA

Example information

Jobs, people, resume variants, and histories are examples. User-created records stay in this browser.

ANALYSIS

Local simulation

The demo uses prepared assessments and local rules. It does not call a live AI model.

ACTIONS

No external submissions

No message, connection request, referral ask, or job application is sent from the demo.

Demo limit: The public build has no live LinkedIn connection, server-side profile, or real job application data.
