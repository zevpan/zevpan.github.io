Clear prototype boundaries

Designed as a product experience, presented as a prototype.

The workflow demonstrates product and process design decisions without implying a live business system.

DATA

Example records

Accounts, opportunities, catalog entries, costs, and company policies are illustrative and stored in this browser.

CONTROL

Human approval

Role views demonstrate review and decision boundaries. They are not production authentication or permissions.

INTEGRATION

No live connections

No live CRM, industrial data source, external model, email, or messaging integration is configured.

Demo limit: Changes stay in the current browser. No customer message is sent, and this portfolio prototype does not claim an employer deployment.
