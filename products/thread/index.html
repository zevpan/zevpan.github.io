One shared thread of context

Memory → Intelligence → Workspace → Human Action

Thread is a team workspace above your systems of record. It keeps the human context that usually gets scattered across inboxes, CRMs, meetings, spreadsheets, and individual memory.

M

Relationship Memory

Persistent context across people, companies, conversations, commitments, previous decisions, overrides, and provenance.

S

Signals

Notice replies, reconnect dates, project signals, stale recommendations, and identity conflicts as the underlying relationship changes.

I

Decision Intelligence

Use deterministic rules for policy and state, then AI where unstructured interpretation genuinely helps.

W

Team Workspace

Bring priority work, evidence, timelines, reconciliation, capacity, and audit into one human decision surface.

Context that compounds

Human control by design

AI proposes. Policy constrains. Humans decide.

Thread does not make high-risk external relationship decisions invisibly. The architecture separates stable business logic from AI interpretation and from human authority.

Human authority
