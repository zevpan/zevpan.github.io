THE INFORMATION DUMP TO PROPOSAL WORKFLOW

Describe the outcome. Inspect completed work. Decide what needs a person.

The flagship flow starts with natural-language notes. It does not force a long form before the system can begin.

“Acme Technologies needs 120 units of Product X next month. They currently pay around $430 per unit and we discussed $410 for the full quantity. They want implementation support and a two-year maintenance agreement. Check availability, prepare the quotation and put together a proposal.”

  1. 01 · STRUCTUREUnderstand the requestIdentify the account, product, quantity, target price, competitor reference, delivery window and service needs.
  2. 02 · VALIDATECheck company contextMatch the catalog, inspect cost and discount rules, show inventory freshness and flag unconfirmed capacity.
  3. 03 · CALCULATEPrepare a governed quoteCompute totals, tax estimate, gross margin and markup. Hold prices that cross policy limits.
  4. 04 · GENERATEPrepare the proposalUse customer requirements, catalog facts and the linked quote version. Keep cost and margin out of the client document.
  5. 05 · REVIEWReturn only decisionsShow exceptions, open assumptions, customer next steps and a manager view built from the same records.
Customer notes→Structured RFQ→Validated quotation→Proposal draft→Human decision
COMMERCIAL RULES AND HUMAN CONTROL

AI interprets context. Deterministic rules calculate the deal.

Pricing that can be inspected

Quote calculations use saved catalog costs, list prices, per-product margin floors, maximum discounts, account currency and example tax rules.

  • ✓Gross margin is calculated as profit divided by selling price.
  • ✓Markup is calculated as profit divided by cost.
  • ✓Quote revisions preserve earlier lines and totals.
  • ✓Policy exceptions are held for the Sales Manager.

Customer documents with clear boundaries

Quotation and proposal previews are built from saved application state and can be downloaded as HTML, then printed or saved as PDF.

  • ✓Confidential internal costs and margins are excluded from customer documents.
  • ✓Draft documents are watermarked until review and approval conditions are met.
  • ✓Availability snapshots and delivery assumptions remain visible.
  • ✓Document versions refer to a specific quotation version.

IMPLEMENTATION EVIDENCE

CUSTOMER REQUEST

Notes to RFQ, quote and proposal

Extract the request, calculate with saved rules, record assumptions, create a quote version and prepare a linked proposal.

COMMERCIAL EXCEPTION

Hold, request approval, record decision

Try a below-floor price, inspect the exception as a manager, approve or reject it and keep the decision on the quote timeline.

Complete routine review and surface attention

Review current operational records, refresh the manager view and separate completed work from approvals, customer actions and blockers.
