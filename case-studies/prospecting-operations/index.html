Transformation objective

Build a relationship-operations layer that converts fragmented network and interaction data into trustworthy state, governed eligibility, transparent priority and a continuously reconciled Action Queue.

StartNew relationship data arrives from professional-network exports, message history, invitation records, research inputs or manual records.
EndA human operator receives a current, evidence-backed and capacity-controlled next action with the correct person, relationship context, rationale and audit trail.

Why the current workflow breaks down

Fragmented relationship dataIdentity, network status, interaction history and account context arrive from different sources and must be reconciled.
State cannot be inferred from connection statusA connection can be new, unanswered, active, nurture, reconnect-later, an opportunity, closed or suppressed.
Human capacity is scarceThousands of records can compete for a finite weekly attention ceiling. The problem is allocation, not maximum send volume.
Actions become staleA new reply can invalidate a follow-up that was correct when the queue was generated.
Spreadsheet operations hit scale limitsFull-dataset scans and repeated joins become increasingly fragile as interaction history grows.
AI needs governanceSemantic interpretation is useful only when recommendations are evidence-backed, bounded by policy and auditable.

Current operating model

  1. 01 · SOURCELinkedIn + other exports
  2. 02 · ORCHESTRATEn8n workflow
  3. 03 · MANAGEGoogle Sheet: People Master + Action Queue
  4. 04 · RECOMMENDAI-assisted next-action analysis
  5. 05 · REVIEWHuman review / export
Diagnosis The core problem is not message generation. It is maintaining a trustworthy relationship state and continuously deciding which human action is justified under limited capacity.

Target operating model

Ingest→Resolve identity→Update relationship memory→Eligibility gate→AI interpretation→Priority + capacity→Action Queue→Human decision→Reconcile

Human vs Automation vs AI

WorkPrimary ownerExamples
Stable structured logicDeterministic automationIdentity keys, deduplication, chronology, DNC, cooldowns, capacity arithmetic, action supersession.
Unstructured relationship meaningAIReconnect intent, message meaning, opportunity signals, relationship summary, next-action suggestion.
External relationship decisionsHumanSending outreach, resolving ambiguity, opportunity qualification, commercial judgement and sensitive overrides.
Control rule AI produces evidence + confidence + proposal. Deterministic policy decides what is allowed. Human authority remains over external communication.

State before priority

The design intentionally separates relationship context from action eligibility.

Relationship states
  • No interaction history
  • Outbound last / Inbound last
  • Active conversation
  • Reconnect later
  • Opportunity
  • Nurture
  • Do Not Contact
  • Closed
Eligibility controls
  • DNC and follow-up blocks
  • Identity resolution
  • Cooldowns
  • Reconnect timing
  • Existing open actions
  • Newer inbound context
  • Active opportunity handling
Design principle State → Eligibility → Priority → Capacity Allocation → Action. AI does not rank prohibited actions first and filter them afterward.

Capacity-aware prioritization

The weekly follow-up ceiling is a maximum, not a target. The system therefore treats operator capacity as a portfolio-allocation problem.

Protected capacityReserve room for inbound replies, newly accepted connections and emergent high-priority cases instead of filling the whole week with generic follow-up.
Transparent rankingExpose priority tier, reason codes, relationship signals and capacity bucket rather than relying on an unexplained black-box score.

Selected requirements

Identity quality firstStable Person IDs, source lineage, deduplication, alias history and a review queue for ambiguous matches.
Evidence-backed AISemantic recommendations must return structured output, confidence and supporting interaction IDs.
Stale-action controlNewer interactions trigger reconciliation and can move an older action to SUPERSEDED.
Fail-closed suppressionDNC and explicit blocks take precedence over AI, ranking and prior actions.
Idempotent workflowsRepeated source imports or reconciliation retries must not duplicate people, interactions or actions.
Decision provenanceHistorical actions retain the state, eligibility, ranking, capacity and AI evidence that justified them.

How value would be measured

Data qualityMatched interactions, duplicate rate, reconciliation backlog and source freshness.
Queue qualityStale-action rate, supersession, dismissal, backlog age and duplicate prevention.
CapacityReserved vs consumed capacity and high-priority cases left unallocated.
AI qualityClassification agreement, evidence accuracy, unsupported claims and human overrides.
Workflow reliabilityRun success, processing latency, retries and resource failures.
Relationship outcomesMeaningful replies, meetings / opportunity progression, reconnect conversion and opt-outs.

The measurement model intentionally separates system health, decision quality, operating efficiency and business outcomes so activity volume is not mistaken for value.

Architecture decision

Build a relationship-operations system, not an autonomous outreach agent.

The system should remove repeated reconciliation, state reconstruction and prioritization work while concentrating human effort on genuine conversations, relationship judgement and commercial decisions.
