Two different automation problems. Two different designs.

Use decision support where judgement matters; deterministic automation where work is repetitive.
Human-in-the-loop warehouseDeterministic finance automation

Warehouse Decision Support

High-runner inbound placement and outbound QC use operational evidence to narrow what needs human judgement.

The system recommends what to review. Warehouse users decide what to do.

Finance Workflow Automation

Large repetitive batches are validated, transformed and entered into one existing finance system automatically.

The target finance system remains the system of record. Humans handle only exceptions.
